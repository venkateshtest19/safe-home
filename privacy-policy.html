Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how Safe Home handles your data.

At Safe Home, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. As an independent affiliate and referral partner of Best Buy, we want to be completely transparent about how your information is handled when you visit our site and make purchases.

We may collect basic information such as your name, email address, and phone number when you voluntarily contact us for pre-purchase guidance, product inquiries, or post-purchase advocacy.
