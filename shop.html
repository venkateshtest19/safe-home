Five Things to Verify Before Buying

1. Operating System

Check whether the product supports your Windows, macOS, Android or iOS version.

2. Device Limit

One-device subscriptions may not cover every computer or phone in your household.

3. Included Features

Confirm which security and privacy tools are actually included in the edition.

4. Renewal Terms

Review the subscription duration, automatic renewal requirements and future charges.

5. Delivery & Returns

Check whether the listing is digital, how the purchase is fulfilled and which retailer return rules apply.
