Refund Policy

Please read our policy carefully regarding refunds for digital software licenses.

At Safe Home, we strive to ensure that every customer is completely satisfied with their purchase. However, because we sell digital products (software license keys) that are delivered instantly via email, our refund policy differs from that of physical goods. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Nature of Digital Products

All products sold on our website are digital software licenses. Once a license key has been generated and emailed to you, it is considered "delivered." Because digital keys cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense, all sales are generally considered final once the key has been sent to your registered email address.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

We understand that technical issues can occur. We will offer a full refund or a replacement license under the following specific circumstances:

  • Invalid or Defective Key: If the license key we provided fails to activate the official software and our support team is unable to resolve the issue.
  • Wrong Product Delivered: If we accidentally send you a license for a different product than the one you purchased (e.g., you bought Norton but received McAfee).
  • Duplicate Purchase: If you accidentally purchased the same license twice in a single transaction, please contact us immediately before the second key is activated.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be granted for the following reasons:

  • Change of Mind: Requests for refunds because you changed your mind or no longer want the product after the key has been delivered.
  • Incompatibility: If you purchase a license for a device that does not meet the software's system requirements (e.g., buying a Windows license for a Mac). It is the customer's responsibility to check system requirements before purchasing.
  • Accidental Purchase: If you purchased the wrong product by mistake after the key has been delivered and viewed.
  • Manufacturer Issues: Issues related to the software manufacturer's servers, regional restrictions, or bugs within the antivirus software itself. (Please contact the manufacturer's official support for software bugs).

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund based on the criteria above, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team within 7 days of your purchase date.
  • Provide your order details, the email address used for the purchase, and a clear explanation of the issue.
  • If applicable, provide screenshots or proof of the activation error.

You can reach our support team by emailing safehome@atomicmail.io or by calling our 24/7 support line.

5. Processing Time

Once we receive your refund request, our team will review it within 24 to 48 hours. If your request is approved, the refund will be processed back to your original payment method. Please allow 5 to 10 business days for the funds to appear in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Chargebacks and Disputes

We ask that you contact us directly to resolve any issues before initiating a chargeback or dispute with your bank or credit card company. Unjustified chargebacks for delivered digital goods may result in the permanent banning of your account and the blacklisting of your license keys by the software manufacturers.
