Refund & Return Policy

Understanding how returns and refunds work through our Best Buy partnership.

At Safe Home, your satisfaction is our top priority. Because we operate as an independent affiliate partner, all financial transactions and purchases are completed directly on BestBuy.com. Therefore, Best Buy's official Return and Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through our links.

1. How Returns and Refunds Work

Since your purchase is processed by Best Buy, any requests for refunds, returns, or exchanges must ultimately be handled through Best Buy's customer service or your Best Buy account. Safe Home does not process financial refunds directly, as we do not handle your payment information.

2. Safe Home's Commitment to You

Even though the transaction occurs on Best Buy, Safe Home stands by our customers. In the event of an unsatisfactory experience, a defective license key, or any issue with your purchase, Safe Home is responsible for making the refund and return process successful. We will actively advocate on your behalf and guide you through Best Buy's return process to ensure a smooth and successful resolution.

3. How to Request Assistance for a Refund

If you need help with a return or refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact Safe Home immediately via phone, email, or our live chat widget.
  • Provide your order details and a clear description of the issue.
  • Our team will guide you on the next steps and, if necessary, escalate the issue to our contacts at Best Buy/Geek Squad to ensure your refund is processed successfully.

4. Digital Products & Change of Mind

Please note that digital software licenses are generally non-refundable once the key has been successfully activated. Refunds based on a "change of mind" after activation are subject to Best Buy's discretion. For software functionality issues or installation errors, you may also reach out to the software manufacturer's official customer care team directly for assistance.
