About Safe Home

Independent antivirus comparisons, practical buying guidance and helpful information to support informed security software purchases.

Who We Are

Safe Home is an independent online product information and referral website focused on consumer antivirus and cybersecurity software.

We help shoppers explore and compare security products from brands such as Norton, McAfee and Bitdefender. Our goal is to make product selection easier by explaining important differences in features, device compatibility, subscription coverage and purchasing considerations.

Safe Home does not manufacture antivirus software or sell software licenses directly. Instead, we provide product information and links to third-party retailer listings where visitors can review current product details and complete purchases if they choose.

How We Operate

Our website features selected antivirus and security software products with descriptions, comparison information and links to relevant Best Buy product listings.

When visitors select a retailer link, they leave Safe Home and visit the retailer's website. Product availability, current pricing, checkout, payment processing, delivery and applicable return policies are handled by the retailer.

Some of our retailer links may be affiliate links. Safe Home may earn a commission from qualifying purchases completed through eligible links. This helps support the operation of our website.

Our content is intended to assist with product research and pre-purchase decisions. We encourage visitors to verify the latest specifications, system requirements, subscription terms and pricing directly with the retailer or manufacturer.
