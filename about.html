How We Operate

Safe Home operates as an independent affiliate and referral partner of Best Buy and its Geek Squad division. We do not directly process payments or act as the primary merchant for the software brands we feature. Instead, we guide our customers to the secure, official Best Buy checkout process. When you click "Buy Now" on our site, you are redirected to Best Buy's dedicated product page. If you complete a purchase, Safe Home earns a small commission at no extra cost to you.

This model ensures that your financial transactions are handled by one of the most trusted retailers in the world, while Safe Home focuses on providing expert guidance, product comparisons, and localized human support.
